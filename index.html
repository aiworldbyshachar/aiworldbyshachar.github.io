Small tools that make everyday work easier.

The apps

ClipBridge

Copy on your Mac. Paste on your PC.

One clipboard for both computers, and the other way around. Text, formatting and images, with one shared history you can search, with no limit. No account and no cloud: the computers talk directly, encrypted.

The one rule: both computers on the same Wi-Fi.
Get ClipBridge Watch the demo Source
FolderBridge

Save on your Mac. Open on your PC.

One folder, kept identical on your Mac and your Windows PC. Put a file in, and it's on the other computer a moment later. No cloud, no account, no storage limit, and nothing is ever lost.

The one rule: both computers on the same home network.
Get FolderBridge Watch the demo Source
KeyBridge

Press it once. It's typed.

Text shortcuts and hotkeys that tell the left and right modifier keys apart: Right Shift + A types your address, Left Shift + A is still just A. They also open links, folders and programs, and they're on every computer you sign in to, Mac or Windows, locked so only you can read them.

The one rule: sign in on each computer. Without signing in, it works fully on that one computer.
